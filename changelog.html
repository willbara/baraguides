    Changelog

    every recommendation change, dated and explained

    When something here changes, this page says what and (more importantly) why. Recommendations move because facts move: a new audit, a pricing change, a policy rewrite, an acquisition. If a change ever seems unexplained, call it out.

    2026-09-04

    Mullvad DNSremoval

    Removed Mullvad DNS from DNS & Network. Mullvad announced it's shutting down its public encrypted DNS servers (dns.mullvad.net) on November 2, 2026, redirecting those resources to sponsor Quad9 instead. Pulling the recommendation now rather than waiting for the cutoff; the category is down to 7 recommendations. This doesn't affect Mullvad VPN itself: DNS resolution through the VPN tunnel uses a separate internal resolver that isn't going anywhere.

    2026-08-17

    Stoatcorrection

    Corrected the jurisdiction claim: Stoat's developer, Revolt Platforms Ltd, is UK-based and answers to UK GDPR and the ICO, not EU law as previously stated. Most data is still hosted on EU servers, but that's a hosting detail, not the governing jurisdiction. Added Mind the bullets noting the UK is a Five Eyes member and that Stoat has no end-to-end encryption — messages are readable on the server. Added a 🇬🇧 uk jurisdiction pill to match, and added it to the site's pill vocabulary since no UK flag existed there before.
