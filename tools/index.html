    Tools

    last updated 2026-08-02 · 25 categories · what changed

    Every category here follows the same format: what actually matters when choosing, a handful of researched picks with honest trade-offs, and a comparison table. No rankings: verdict tags tell you who each tool is for, not which one "wins".

    not sure where to start? Don't pick tools first. Read threat modeling, figure out what you're actually protecting, then come back, or grab a complete setup and skip the decisions entirely.

    how these pages work

    Verdict tags, not rankings. "The default pick" means it's the right answer for most people; "the anonymity pick" means it's the right answer for a specific threat model. A tool further down a page isn't worse, it's for someone else.

    Every entry lists what to mind, not just what's good. If a page here reads like marketing, that's a bug: report it.

    Dates matter. Each page shows when it was last reviewed, and the changelog explains every change of heart.
