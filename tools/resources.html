    ~/tools/resources

    Resources

    last updated 2026-08-02 · 12 resources · what changed

    Not every useful thing is an "install this tool" recommendation. External sites worth bookmarking: breach checkers, terms-of-service reviewers, and free utility collections that don't fit the format of the rest of this site, but earn a place in the bookmarks bar anyway.

    independent third parties These are sites run by other people and organizations, not by this project. Judge them on their own track record: link rot, ownership changes, and shifting business models happen to bookmarked sites just like anywhere else.

    breach & exposure checking

    breach lookup

    DataBreach.com

    A breach notification and lookup service: check whether your data has surfaced in a known breach.

     breach lookup

    haveibeenpwned.com

    The original, most trusted breach-checking database. It also offers email notifications for future breaches.

     disposable inbox

    temp-mail.org

    A disposable inbox for one-off signups. It's much weaker than a real alias service: these addresses are public and guessable, and you lose the inbox forever. Use it only for truly throwaway, one-time signups.

    research & comparison

    independent guide

    privacyguides.org

    The project this site's format draws inspiration from. An excellent independent companion resource with its own deep tool comparisons.

     crypto / no-kyc

    kycnot.me

    A database of cryptocurrency services that don't require identity verification (KYC).

     curated collection

    privacypack.org

    Another curated privacy tool collection, worth cross-referencing against this site's own picks.

     policy reviewer

    privacyspy.org

    A terms-of-service and privacy-policy reviewer and rating site: it reads the fine print so you don't have to.

     policy reviewer

    tosdr.org

    "Terms of Service; Didn't Read": crowd-reviewed, rated summaries of services' terms of service and privacy policies.

     hardening guides

    digital-defense.io

    Digital security and privacy hardening guides, for going deeper than a single tool recommendation.

     search engine comparison

    searchengine.party

    A tool that compares popular search engines across privacy-related data points.

     VPN comparison

    vpn.techlore.tech

    More extensive VPN comparisons, from Techlore.

     messaging comparison

    securemessagingapps.com

    An informational guide (not a messaging service) that compares secure messaging apps by encryption, data collection, and transparency; check its methodology before trusting the verdicts.

    general collections & utilities

    community collection

    fmhy.net

    A large, community-maintained collection of free media and tool resources covering far more than just privacy.

     file conversion

    vert.sh

    Local, in-browser file format conversion: your files never get uploaded to a server.
